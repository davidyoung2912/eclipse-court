# Improvement plan 2: clarity, depth and longevity

Verification per phase: `npm run test`, `npm run lint`, `npx tsc -b`, `npm run build` (no browser testing). One commit per phase; stop after each so the context can be cleared.

## A. Quality of life

1. **Threat overlay + odds preview** – a settings/board toggle tints squares the opponent could attack next turn; hovering or selecting an attack target shows the expected duel odds (from `matchup()`).
2. **Duel speed + skip** – a duel animation speed setting (normal / fast / instant) and a per-duel skip.
3. **Skirmish presets** – save, name, load and delete rule combinations on the setup screen.
4. **Save/resume everywhere** – resume for a Gauntlet fight in progress, puzzles and mail games, not just the one skirmish slot.
5. **AI "why" line** – an optional one-line reason after each AI move, drawn from the personality and the search's best-scoring factor.
6. **Suggested next step** – a "Suggested" row on the main menu after onboarding (unsolved puzzles in the current tier, next scenario, unplayed daily).

## B. New modes and fun

7. **Boss foes** – named ladder/campaign bosses with a signature rule (extra cast per turn, hazard start cells, ...), data-driven.
8. **Mayhem mutators** – optional chaos modifiers for skirmish (mid-game cell spawns, spell steals, piece swaps) with a Shard bonus.
9. **Seasonal rotating events** – a limited-time modifier on the daily/weekly games.
10. **Spectate links** – a watch link that replays a finished online game move by move.
11. **Co-op scenarios** – two players share the Solari side against a scripted AI (hot-seat first, online after).

## C. Longevity

12. **Endless generated puzzles** – expose the miner/solver as an endless, rated puzzle mode.
13. **Gauntlet meta-progression** – unlockable starting relics, a daily seeded run with its own streak.
14. **Legacy army** – carry Gauntlet veterans, perks and relics into skirmish as an optional army.
15. **Ladder season rewards** – season-end Shards by rank, a season banner cosmetic, a season history screen.
16. **Piece mastery goals** – per-role challenges ("win 10 duels with a Bruiser in the dark") with cosmetic rewards.
17. **Cosmetics depth** – rarer, pricier animated tiers (piece sets, board effects).

## D. AI and balance

18. **AI character** – behaviours beyond weights (baiter, Leader-guard) and a distinct Grandmaster tier; run the sim scripts across all rule pairings.

## E. Accessibility and reach

19. **Screen-reader move log** – `aria-live` announcements for moves, duels and spells; keyboard coverage for spells and the draft.
20. **Wide-screen layout** – board and tray side by side on tablets and desktops.
21. **String extraction** – move inline UI text behind a lookup so localization is possible later.

## Status

Phase 1 done: the Danger toggle (Settings and in-game button) rings own pieces under threat and shows attack odds on enemy pieces; off under fog. Phase 2 done: a Duel speed setting (Normal, Fast, Instant) and a Skip button in every live duel that plays the rest out with the duel AI at the auto-duel skill. Phase 3 done: My presets on the Skirmish setup screen save, name, load and delete up to 12 rule, army, board and twist combinations (kept in `eclipse-court:presets`, so backups include them). Phase 4 done: an unfinished Gauntlet fight and an unfinished puzzle or Puzzle Rush are kept (`eclipse-court:saved-fight`, `eclipse-court:saved-puzzle`, left out of backups) and offered as Resume Fight on the Gauntlet and Resume Puzzle on the Puzzles screen; games by link already persist per game in the mail list. Phase 5 done: an AI reasons setting (Settings, Play aids) shows one italic line under the objective explaining the AI's latest move while it is your turn, written by `explainMove` in `src/ai/why.ts` from the position (attack odds, beacon, retreat of the wounded, light, spell) in the foe's personality voice. Phase 6 done: a Suggested section on the main menu (after the tutorial, hidden while First steps shows) lists up to three next steps from `suggestions` in `menuModel.ts`: unplayed daily match, daily puzzle and weekly, then the first tier with unsolved puzzles, then the next uncleared scenario. Phase 7 done: three ladder bosses (`src/engine/data/bosses.ts`: the Veil Sister, the Ash Tyrant, the Hollow King, rated 1650–1800) unlock once the peak rating reaches 1500 and are listed under Bosses on the Ladder. Each adds a signature rule through match settings; the new `freeCast` setting lets a court's first cast each turn leave the turn going. Campaign bosses keep their existing rule text. Phase 8 done: three Mayhem mutators under Twists on the Skirmish setup (`src/engine/rules/mayhem.ts`, `config.mayhem`): Cell spawns (a mirrored pair of cells every 3 rounds), Spell thefts (a spell leaps between Leaders every 5 rounds) and Piece swaps (two same-role pieces trade places every 4 rounds), all decided by the seed and applied after each round; the dial notes what happened, and each adds 10% to the Shard multiplier. Phase 9 done: seasonal events (`src/engine/data/events.ts`) run 10 days of every fortnight (Wild Moon, Thieves’ Season, Masquerade, Hard Frost, Mist Festival), laid over the daily match and the weekly match (the weekly keeps its Monday’s event all week) through `withEvent`; they use the existing mayhem, sudden death and fog rules, so the Shard multiplier rises with no extra code, and the Daily and Weekly screens name the event. Phase 10 done: watch links (`#w=…`, `watchLink` in `src/share.ts`) open a finished game in the Replay screen as a spectator (autoplay, a “Watching a finished game” line, no Play On From Here); an online game's end panel offers Copy Watch Link, and the Paste a Code screen accepts them. Phase 11 done: a Co-op tab on the Campaign (`src/engine/data/coop.ts`, six scenarios with ids starting `coop-`) lets two people share the Solari court on one device against the scripted AI. The first player moves on odd rounds and the second on even ones, with a pass screen before each Solari turn (switchable), Player 1/2 turn banners and a Player line under the objective. Stars use the scenario star table under the `coop-` ids, so solo scenario counts and achievements are unchanged, and each tab has its own reset; online co-op is not built. Phase 12 done: Endless Puzzles (Puzzles screen, `src/endless.ts`) generate rated puzzles on the spot with the miner's generator and solver, now shared in `src/sim/generate.ts` (the `mine` script uses it too). A Web Worker (`src/endlessWorker.ts`) searches while the screen is read and the previous puzzle is played; three bands (Gentle, Steady, Sharp) follow your Elo-style rating, which a solve raises and a miss lowers (one try each, 2 Shards per solve, record kept in `eclipse-court:endless`). Endless puzzles are not resumable and do not count towards the authored puzzle achievements. Phase 13 done: the daily run keeps a streak (`liveStreak` in `src/ascension.ts`; consecutive days won, best streak, +2 Shards per earlier day up to 5, on top of the 30), and the Gauntlet screen offers a Starting relic: one relic unlocks per gauntlet run won (`RELIC_UNLOCK_WINS`, `startingRelics`), and the chosen one begins the run in `relics`. Daily runs take no starting relic. Phase 14 done: a finished Gauntlet run (won, lost or abandoned) that has a veteran leaves its army as a Legacy (`src/engine/data/legacy.ts`, stored by `src/legacy.ts` in `eclipse-court:legacy`; a weaker run never replaces a stronger one by `legacyPower`). Skirmish setup offers Legacy under Army against the AI (not with two players or online): the carried pieces keep rank, perks and HP, the Leader keeps the run's spells (those the court can cast), relic rules apply and growth is on, while the AI drafts its own army. Legacy matches carry no extra Shard bonus. Phase 15 done: ending a ladder season (`startSeason` in `src/ladder.ts`) now returns a `SeasonReward` and the Ladder screen pays it: Shards by the rank the season's peak reached (`SEASON_SHARDS`, 10 for Squire up to 150 for Eclipse Champion), shown in the confirm and in a result card. The latest finished season flies a rank-coloured banner under the tiles, and a Season history fold lists every past season with its peak, rank and Shards (`seasonHistory`, derived from `past`, so older saves read back too). Phase 16 done: piece goals (Stats, Piece goals; `src/engine/data/roleGoals.ts`, store `src/roleGoals.ts` in `eclipse-court:role-goals`) count duels each role wins, split by the light on the square, from every counted game (`duelTally` replays the record). Each of the seven roles has four goals (wins, wins in the dark, wins in the light, a larger win count; Leader and Aura targets are lower) that pay 15–40 Shards when claimed, and completing 6, 14 and 28 goals earns the Comet and Supernova capture effects and the Laurel codex frame (`goals` on a cosmetic: earned with `grant`, never sold). Phases 17 onward not started.
